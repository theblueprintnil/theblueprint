# Brand Voice — The Blueprint

The voice guide for all public-facing content. It is distilled from the
existing homepage, about, brands, and compliance pages so new content sounds
like the same product, not a rewrite.

## One-line identity

The Blueprint is the two-sided NIL operating platform: athletes build verified
personal brands; brands run compliant campaigns — deals, contracts, and
disclosures in one audited workspace.

## Voice pillars

| Pillar | What it means | In practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decisive | States what the product does without hedging | Short sentences. "Build your NIL brand. Manage your NIL business." |
| Concrete | Shows specifics instead of vibes | Named features: media kit, deal pipeline, disclosure checkpoints, audit trail, guardian approval queue |
| Trust-forward | Talks about security early and honestly | Verification, private storage, least-privilege access, append-only audit events |
| Athlete-first | The athlete owns the brand | Athletes control what sponsors see, who has access, and what gets published |
| Compliance-aware | Never overpromises on rules | "We help you stay organized — we never make eligibility determinations for you." |

## Do

- Lead with the outcome for the reader, then the feature that delivers it.
- Use the platform vocabulary (see Terminology) so copy stays consistent.
- Quantify when you have real facts; use aspirational framing otherwise
  ("two-sided platform", "verified brand"), never fake numbers.
- Keep CTAs imperative and specific: "Start your trial", "Browse athletes",
  "Read the compliance guidelines".
- Acknowledge the human-in-the-loop: approvals are human decisions, AI assists
  but never decides alone.

## Do not

- Do not claim NIL eligibility or compliance outcomes ("NCAA-approved",
  "school-compliant"). Say "disclosure-ready", "organized for review".
- Do not give legal, tax, or eligibility advice. Refer to qualified
  professionals and the platform's non-legal role.
- Do not promise features outside the MVP (white-label, API, custom reports,
  advanced analytics are Release 4; premium modules are plans, not built
  features).
- Do not invent statistics. The homepage stats band is illustrative demo copy;
  public pages must not present unverified numbers as real.
- Do not use hype language: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "the #1",
  "guaranteed ROI".
- Do not use exclamation points. The punctuation is a period.

## Headline pattern

Existing heroes follow this pattern:

> Eyebrow (kicker) → H1 claim with a brand-gradient accent → one-sentence
> description → two CTAs (primary trial, secondary browse/learn) → microcopy
> qualifier.

Examples already on the site:

- "Build your NIL brand. / Manage your NIL *business.*"
- "Find athletes worth / *every deal.*"
- "The operating system for / *athlete brands and brand deals.*"

## Terminology glossary

| Term | Use this meaning |
| --- | --- |
| NIL | Name, image, and likeness. Rules differ by NCAA policy, school, conference, state, and federal law; the platform is not the authority. |
| Media kit | The athlete-controlled, shareable brand package: bio, highlights, audience numbers, portfolio, partnership categories, links. |
| Verification | Identity-confirmation steps taken on the platform. It is not an eligibility or compliance determination. |
| Disclosure | Required-tag workflows and reminders. The platform does not file disclosures. |
| Guardian consent | Parent/legal-guardian approval required for minors' commercial activity. Automated processes never bypass it. |
| Deal pipeline | Received → qualified → athlete review → negotiation → contract review → approval → signed → fulfilled → closed. |
| Compliance guardrails | State/school/conference rule reminders, disclosure checkpoints, audit trail. |
| Trial | 7-day trial, credit card required, cancel anytime, auto-converts to paid unless canceled. |
| Tenant/workspace | Each business (athlete business, agency) is a tenant with isolated data. Internal term; use "workspace" publicly. |
| Audit trail / audit-ready | Append-only event history for offers, approvals, contracts, and document access. |

## Personas

- **Athlete** — competitive student-athlete building a personal brand; worried
  about staying eligible and keeping control of their image. Cares about:
  verified profile, media kit, sponsorship readiness, approvals.
- **Guardian / parent** — manages a minor athlete's business; cares about:
  consent, documents, signed agreements, permission controls, transparency.
- **Agency / representation** — runs an athlete roster and deal workflow; cares
  about: intake, pipeline, contracts, approvals, reporting, auditability.
- **Brand / sponsor** — wants to run compliant campaigns with credible athletes;
  cares about: verified directory, campaign tooling, contract + deliverable
  management, disclosure records.

When writing a page, name the primary persona in the brief and address that
persona directly in the body copy; keep secondary personas visible through
subsections and FAQ.
